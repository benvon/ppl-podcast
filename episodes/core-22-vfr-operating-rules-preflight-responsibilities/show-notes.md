# VFR Operating Rules and Preflight Responsibilities

**Episode:** 22
**Version:** 0.1.3

## In this episode

Elena and Jonah prepare a daytime VFR trip. A blank installed display leads Elena to change airplanes. A destination runway closure leads her to choose another landing airport and recalculate fuel. En route, slower groundspeed leads her to use a prepared fuel stop. The lesson connects pilot-in-command responsibility, airworthiness, current preflight information, passenger restraints, and altitude choices to those decisions.
